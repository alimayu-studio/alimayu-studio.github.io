Field Notes · ABIDE with FAAITH

Where Faith, Public Service, and Practical Wisdom Meet in Uncertain Times

Faith communities have long been places where people find help, hope, and each other. In uncertain times, that role grows.

By Dr. Allison Alimayu · November 17, 2026 · 2 minute read

Long before many public programs existed, congregations fed people, visited the sick, and stood with families in hard seasons. That work continues, often quietly.

Why Faith Communities Matter

  • They are trusted, especially where public institutions are not.
  • They know families over years, not just one visit.
  • They show up in person, at home, and in crisis.

Practical Wisdom for Uncertain Times

  • Start with presence: listening comes before programs.
  • Partner, do not duplicate: connect families to services that already exist.
  • Keep families at the center, with respect for their choices.

Where Public Service Fits

Public agencies and faith leaders can do more together than apart. Clear roles, shared training, and respect for faith traditions make those partnerships work.
