Anchored Leadership: Staying Grounded When Leading Through Institutional Change

Institutions change, sometimes faster than the people in them can absorb. Anchored leaders keep their footing by holding to what does not change.

By Dr. Allison Alimayu · November 17, 2026 · 2 minute read

Funding shifts. Leaders turn over. Policies change overnight. Leading through that kind of change asks for something steadier than a strategy.

What Anchors a Leader

  • Clear values: knowing what will not be traded away.
  • Daily practice: prayer, reflection, or quiet time that comes before the inbox.
  • Trusted people: a few voices who will say the hard thing kindly.

Leading Others Through Change

  • Say what is known, what is not, and when more will be known.
  • Protect the people closest to the work.
  • Keep the mission in view, even when the plan changes.

Rest Is Part of the Work

Leaders who never rest make worse decisions. Sabbath, in whatever form it takes, is not a break from leadership. It is part of it.
