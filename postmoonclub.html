Postmoon Club

Postpartum lasts a full year. Her care should, too.

The Postmoon Club is a postpartum health and wellness company for the 12 months after birth. The focus is the mother, not the baby.

The Mail Club

Bath and Soak Blends

Herbal sitz bath bags for the early months, then soaks made for her alone. A separate line, coming after the mail club.

The Moon Circle

A Moon Circle is the group of people around a mother for all 12 months. They check in on her, and they check in on each other.

Anyone can help. At its heart, this is women helping women, because women's health gets ignored far too often.

Who Is Behind It

The Postmoon Club is led by Allison Alimayu, PhD, with graduate-level training in health promotion and lived experience as a postpartum mother. It is an initiative of Alimayu Studio.
