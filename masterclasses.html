Running for office

From the decision to run through election night, taught by someone who has done it.

Dr. Alimayu won an open seat on the Nevada Board of Regents in 2012, ran for US Congress in 2018, and has trained candidates with Emerge Nevada, the Black Democratic Empowerment Project, and the Southern Nevada Black Elected Officials. This masterclass turns that experience into a clear, step-by-step course that anyone can take for free.
