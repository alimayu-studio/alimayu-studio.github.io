System design and risk mitigation

For a national research funder, she designed a governance system that names six risks to outcomes and builds a control for each

Risk identifiedControl she designed
Priorities chosen informally, with no documented process→Documented selection: three intake pathways, agreed allocations, and senior leadership sign-off
Investments drift from the problem they were meant to solve→Alignment worksheet and impact workbook at every gate, creating an auditable record
Teams selected for credentials over capacity to deliver→A team gate as the first competitive filter, before any proposal is written
Narrow, repeat review panels→A new, independent, diverse panel at each gate
Inconsistent rules on IP, data, AI, and conflicts of interest→One standing agreement that settles those terms across every funded consortium
Funding ends with no transition plan→A funded six-month off-ramp; no exit until a named steward is ready
Stage gates: capital narrows as evidence grows

Prize24 months
A forward-only review rule credits candid reporting of failure, so problems surface early instead of late.
