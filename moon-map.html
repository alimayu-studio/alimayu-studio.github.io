Your Postmoon Map

Healing takes about a year. Plan who will be there for you in each of your twelve moons.

Your map stays on this device. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere.

1

Your Year

Your birth date sets the dates for each moon. A due date works too.

Want to see how it works first?
2

Your Circle

The people who can help this year. Three or four is enough. One person can take many moons.

3

Your Twelve Moons

For each moon, choose who is on and what they cover.

4

Your Hard Day Plan

Decide this now, while things are calm. Share it with your circle.

When you text it, your circle knows it means come now, no questions.

Save and Share

Save your map as an image for your phone. Then send each person their moons.

Tip: start a Moon Circle group chat. Make one group in Messages or WhatsApp with everyone on your map. Name it "Moon Circle." Paste the plan in as the first message, so everyone sees who has which moon.

Download image

On a phone, press and hold the image to save it to your photos. It fits as a lock screen.
